spec_min_p sweep: the optimum MOVES per model (IQ3_XXS vs Q2_0 are opposites)
==============================================================================
The speculative window T is gated by the previous draft's confidence:
    T starts at 1; it grows toward spec only while dprob[T-1] >= spec_min_p.
A high-quality drafter can afford a high bar; a mediocre drafter must cast wide.
